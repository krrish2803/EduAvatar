# Problem Statement

## The Challenge

Traditional education faces several critical problems:

### 1. One-Size-Fits-All Education
- Classrooms teach at a fixed pace, leaving struggling students behind and boring advanced ones
- No adaptation to individual learning styles, languages, or knowledge levels
- Teachers cannot simultaneously address 30+ different learning needs

### 2. Lack of Personalization
- Students receive the same explanations regardless of their background
- No memory of past learning sessions or progress
- No adjustment for language preferences or cultural context

### 3. Passive Learning
- Students watch videos or read without interaction
- No diagnostic questions to verify understanding
- No immediate feedback or misconception correction

### 4. Scalability Issues
- Qualified tutors are expensive and scarce
- Language barriers limit access to quality education
- No 24/7 availability for learning

### 5. Assessment Gaps
- Traditional tests happen after learning, not during
- No real-time understanding verification
- No adaptive difficulty based on performance
