Rules for High-Quality Interactive Diagrams:
- Design the diagram so it can be built or revealed step-by-step (never dump the complete complex diagram at once).
- Synchronize every visual change with the teacher's spoken explanation.
- Use highlighting, color changes, arrows, zooming, or sequential appearance to guide attention.
- Keep the design clean, high-contrast, and readable on video.
- For complex systems, use progressive disclosure (show high-level first, then details).
